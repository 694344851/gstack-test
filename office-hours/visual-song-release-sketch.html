decision runway

1. content analyst

pulls a short synopsis, identifies conflict, symbolic motifs, and who the song is emotionally "for".

2. lyric planner

maps verse / pre-chorus / chorus against the emotion curve before writing a single line.

3. arrangement call

bpm 92, d minor, wide chorus lift, taiko-style low percussion, strings in final hook.

4. cover director

turns the mood board into a poster-like single cover instead of a generic AI image.

5. render + branch

generates the audio, then forks alternative style directions without losing the same story thesis.
